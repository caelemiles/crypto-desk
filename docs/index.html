What it has learned

Paper money only. The bot learns like a day trader: a playbook of setups, practised on past charts every 6 hours and traded only after passing an exam on unseen weeks. About 2% of equity at risk per trade (half while a setup is on probation), 15-minute candles from Binance, runs about every 10 minutes on GitHub Actions. Protections pause trading after 3 losses in a row or a 6% losing day. Past results do not guarantee future ones.
